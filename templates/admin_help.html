GUIDES / SUPPORT

Help & support

Change your password

  1. Open Settings & privacy → Credentials.
  2. Verify your current password.
  3. Enter the current password again, then the new password twice.
  4. Select Update credentials.

Client review-request flow

  1. The client builds a solution and submits it for review.
  2. Marketing reviews pricing, scope and proposal details.
  3. Technical reviews quantities, materials, surveys and the bill of materials.
  4. The superadmin performs final approval.
  5. The client receives the approved result through their account and message thread.

Need assistance?

Contact the VTIC system administrator and include the page, time, and a screenshot of the issue.
